LAN Share notices for and0null.lan-share 1.2.0.

This package vendors the oma.nearby 1.1.2 helper binary
(payload/bin/narciss-lan-share-helper, pinned x86_64 build) and ports its
Model.js shaping helpers into overlays/modules/widgets/nearby/NearbyModel.js.
The helper speaks the LocalSend protocol on port 53317. This mod is an
independent integration; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by LocalSend.
